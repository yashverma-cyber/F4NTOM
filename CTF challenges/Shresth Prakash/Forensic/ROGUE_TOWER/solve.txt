Step:1 -->First Filter the UDP Protocols.

Step:2 -->Look for the port no. 55000. 

Step:3 -->Right Click on it ->follow ->UDP stream(shortcut --> ctrl+alt+shift+U)

Step:4 -->In the right corner of the stream section increase the no. untill you don't get "UNAUTHORIZED-TEST-NETWORK PLMN=00101 CELLID=91521 ".

Step:5 -->Remember the CELLID.

Step:6 -->Then filter the HTTP Protocols.

Step:7 -->use  ctrl+alt+shift+H to see the HTTP stream.

Step:8 -->In the right corner of the stream section increase the no. untill you don't get the pieces of Base64 encoding.

Step:9 -->Combine all the pieces of the Base64 encoding then open Cyberchef and paste on it.

Step:10 -->Again filter the HTTP Protocols.

Step:11 -->Look for the all GET request.

Step:12 -->Right Click on it ->follow ->HTTP stream.

Step:13 -->Increase the stream no. Unit find that type of Request
                 
                -GET /api/register HTTP/1.1
                 Host: network.carrier.com
                 User-Agent: MobileDevice/1.0 (IMSI:310410050746829; CELL:91521)
                 Accept: */*
                 Connection: close

Step:14 -->Now copy the IMSI no. except the starting of 6 digits.

Step:15 -->Now go back to the cyberchef ,select "From base64 and XOR"

Step:16 -->In XOR change HEX to UTF8.

Step:17 -->Then Paste your IMSI no.

Step:18 -->Click on Bake ,You will get the FLAG.
